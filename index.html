Questions

Is the data real?

Yes. Tokens are checked against the Solana blockchain, and names, logos, market caps and charts are live from DexScreener and GeckoTerminal. Bets settle on the real market cap at the moment they expire.

Is this real money?

Not yet. Stakes are demo SOL, and every new player starts with 1 demo SOL. Every other player is a real visitor, and bets, balances and settlements are kept in cubet's shared bet book on our server. No wallet connects and nothing is sent on-chain, so there are no transactions to show yet. The Proof page lists every settlement with the market data it used.

How does a bet work?

Pick a coin, a target market cap, higher or lower, and a deadline. Someone takes the other side with the same stake. Whoever is right when time runs out takes the pot.

Which coins can I bet on?

Any Solana token with a trading pair. Paste its contract address, or pick one from the live markets.

How is a bet settled?

When a bet ends, our server reads the token's market cap at that exact minute from its GeckoTerminal price candle (the live DexScreener cap if it ended in the last two minutes) and pays the winner the whole pot. Each settlement on the Proof page links the pool and candle so you can check it. If no market data exists for the expiry, the bet is void and both stakes are refunded.

Who can see my bets?

Everyone sees open bets, bets in play and settlements, under an anonymous handle like 4xRw…LhgK. No account, email or wallet is needed. Your session is a cookie in this browser.
