Everything cubet says it does, the live check behind it, and what it doesn't do yet.

What's real, and what isn't yet

Real

  • Every token is checked on-chain. Our server calls Solana getAccountInfo on the address and only accepts SPL Token or Token-2022 mints.
  • Live market data. Names, logos, market caps and 24h change come from DexScreener. Charts and settlement prices come from GeckoTerminal price candles.
  • Every player is a real visitor. Bets are matched in one shared bet book on our server. No bots or sample players.
  • Settlement on the real market cap. When a bet ends, the server reads the market cap at that exact minute, pays the winner, and records the pool and candle it used, listed below.

Not yet

  • Stakes are demo SOL. Balances are numbers in cubet's database. No wallet connects and nothing is sent on-chain, so there are no bet transactions to show.
  • Settlement is run by cubet's server, not by an on-chain program. You can recompute every result from the public candle it links, but you are trusting our server to run it.
  • No cubet token. cubet has not launched a token. Any token claiming to be cubet's is not ours unless its address appears on this page and on @cubetsol_.
  • Market cap is DexScreener's figure for the token's top pair (FDV when no market cap is published). A past market cap is that pair's candle close price times the same supply ratio.
